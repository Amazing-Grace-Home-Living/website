Mission rules and controls

Unlock Nexus by passing Foundations (3 of 4) and completing a practice milestone listed in Learn & Play: Match Maker, Matrix Academy, a legacy Matrix session, or the RPG Echo Gardens region. Clear Nexus to unlock the tactics lesson, then continue through Syndicate and Boss. Pass the sovereignty lesson after Boss to unlock Act IV and waves 10–12.

Choose a tower, then tap a cell off the path to place it. Tap an existing tower to upgrade it. Keyboard: select a tower with Tab and Enter, Tab to the board, use arrows to choose a cell, then Enter to place or upgrade. Start waves when ready; you can build between or during waves.

Restarting or changing stages resets the mission budget and core. Vault upgrades apply to the next mission attempt. A mission ends when its final wave clears or the core reaches zero.

Place defensive turrets along the infiltration grid to protect the Core.

Credits: 150
Core: 20
Wave: 1
Points: 0

Choose your foundational directive

Your choice shapes this mission and its ending. Choose before building; restart to explore another path. None is a universal moral answer.

Field guide · observe, decide, reflect

The two routes converge at the central nexus, then split before the core. A new attempt starts with 500 credits and 85 of 100 integrity, before Vault bonuses. Diplomacy restores the missing 15. Ordinary breaches cost 5 integrity; the Vanguard costs 20; Siphons cost none.

Amber + tiles gain 30% fire rate for six seconds, then red × tiles black out for three seconds. Surges recur every fifteen seconds of active simulation. Aegis Nodes protect turrets within 65 pixels from blackouts and the Vanguard’s nearby disable pulse, and add 15% attack power. Auras do not stack. The Phase Prism pierces enemies in a straight line.

Data Siphons steal 5 credits each second and reduce damage to nearby allies by 35%. The Vanguard adapts to the most frequent incoming hit type: Laser, Explosive, or EMP. Its label shows its current 60% resistance. EMP slows, Plasma explodes, and other direct-fire towers use Laser damage.

After each wave: Who benefited from your directive? Who bore its cost? What evidence would change your plan? The ending is a story outcome, not proof of a universally correct moral choice.
