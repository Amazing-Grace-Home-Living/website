PRACTICAL ENDPOINT ENGINEERING

Apply the concepts safely

Suggested lab in an authorized test tenant. Use test devices and test accounts; do not change production roles or compliance settings without approval.

  1. Reporting: Compare a device compliance report with a device inventory view. Document what each one reveals and who should have access.
  2. Delegation: Draft an RBAC matrix for a help-desk role and a regional administrator. Explain the scope tags and permissions each role needs.
  3. Tenant health: Write a simple readiness checklist covering service health, alerts, audit logs, and an incident escalation path.
