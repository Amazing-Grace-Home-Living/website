How credentials are handled
Sign in to your own official provider account. Create a scoped key, then paste it only into the hidden HOST terminal prompt. The encrypted vault is at ~/.grace-remote/vault.json, outside Git. Browser tokens and provider keys are separate. Revoke or rotate credentials at the provider; locking this page only clears its session token.
