How to play · keyboard controls and decisions

Swap two adjacent gems to match three or more. Reach every displayed mission objective, then answer its knowledge questions. Invalid swaps are free. Keyboard: Tab into the board, arrow keys move focus, Enter or Space selects gems, Escape clears a selection.

Optional decisions: System Break adds 15 awareness and costs 10 integrity; Architect Move adds 5 awareness and restores 10 integrity. Choose once before each successful swap. Stats stay between 0 and 100 and are fictional game values.
