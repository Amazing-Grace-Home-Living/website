HOW TO PLAY

Tap two neighbors or swipe a gem. Match 3 or more of the same symbol. Reach the target before your moves run out.

  • 4 in a lineClear a row or column ↔
  • 5 in a lineA supernova clears its color ✦
  • T or L shapeA burst clears the nearby 3 × 3 ✹

Special gems activate when matched. Effects can set off other specials. Invalid swaps cost no moves.
